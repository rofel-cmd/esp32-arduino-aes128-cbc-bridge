# Limitations

- Encryption is not authenticated and does not prevent replay.
- The included key and Bluetooth PIN are intentionally public demonstration values.
- The six-command run is a smoke test, not an endurance or statistical reliability study.
- Round-trip times are firmware-reported and were not measured with synchronized external instrumentation.
- Range, interference, through-wall operation, brownout behavior, and simultaneous cold start were not qualified.
- The September 11 malformed-response event was not byte-for-byte diagnosed because the rejected bytes were not printed.
- Loaded firmware identity was not independently read back during the September 14 run; the record identifies the checkout and prior upload evidence.
- The solderless prototype and schematic are educational artifacts, not a production or safety-rated design.
